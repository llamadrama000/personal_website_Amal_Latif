InsureMEP AI Onboarding Capstone — Report & Documentation Lead

January - June 2026

AI Rental Market Intelligence Dashboard

Spring 2026 · Deep Learning & Applications · Team of 4

  • Built an autonomous LLM-powered rental monitoring system: RentCast API live listings, rule-based deal scoring ($/sqft vs. ZIP median), and GPT-4o-mini market briefs running every 15 minutes via APScheduler.
  • Agentic pipeline (Fetch → Score → Store → Detect → Analyze → Update) with zero user intervention; separated scoring logic from LLM narration to eliminate hallucination risk.
  • Tech stack: Python · GPT-4o-mini · RentCast API · SQLite · Streamlit

Salary Premium of AI Skills in Supply Chain

Spring 2026 · AI Impact on Firms, Society & Economy · Team of 5

  • Analyzed 123,849 LinkedIn job postings (2023–2024); found AI-skill salary gap of $53,927 (Welch's t = 22.72, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = 1.04).
  • Built a binary AI-skill indicator via keyword matching; normalized salary formats; applied regression to control for seniority.

Understanding Premium Conversion on High Note — Causal Analytics

Winter 2026 · Customer & Social Analytics · Team of 4

  • Applied propensity score matching, logistic regression, and regression discontinuity design to isolate the causal effect of peer networks on free-to-premium conversion; found a 9.1 pp treatment effect (p < 0.001), OR = 1.134 per additional premium friend.
